# Reports and Evidence

Reviewed run evidence is indexed in [evidence/README.md](evidence/README.md).

Engineering reports are generated from WSL Ubuntu execution evidence. Codex runs and analyzes Verilator, ModelSim, XSim, and vendor CLI jobs; raw logs, waves, databases, and generated reports stay under `$RUN_ROOT`.

Only reviewed and sanitized material belongs here, such as:

- normalized build metrics and concise pass/fail summaries;
- milestone reports that cite commands, source revision, tool version, and evidence identifiers;
- board-test results with private paths, host data, and license information removed;
- portfolio summaries grounded in confirmed engineering evidence.

Windows Work may turn the reviewed material into polished portfolio documents, PDFs, slides, and interview narratives. It must not invent metrics, replace the canonical engineering report, or use unreviewed raw output as a final claim.

Use [the milestone template](milestones/MILESTONE_TEMPLATE.md) and [portfolio summary template](portfolio/PROJECT_SUMMARY_TEMPLATE.md) as starting points.
