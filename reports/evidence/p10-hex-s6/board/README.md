# P10 HEX S6 board photographs

`B0.jpg` through `B8.jpg` are Owner-provided original-byte photographs of the
final firmware-driven board sequence. Their SHA-256 values and the associated
state interpretation are recorded in `../result.json` and `../summary.md`.

The images establish visible board behavior only. They do not independently
prove the programming command, the private build configuration, analog timing,
or electrical transient behavior.
